Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting started with IN-CRM.

Does the system require complex programming integration?

No! Just scan the QR code as if you are opening WhatsApp Web, and the system will instantly organize your messages without any technical expertise needed.

Can multiple employees reply from a single number?

Yes, your entire team can log into the system and reply from the same WhatsApp number, and the system will auto-distribute the chats among them.

Is my number at risk of being banned?

The system is programmed with smart time delays between automated replies, making it completely safe and simulating human behavior.

How does the "Chat Pulling" feature work if an agent delays?

You set a specific timeframe. If the agent doesn't reply within that time, the system automatically pulls the chat from them and routes it to another available online agent.
